NexusFlow migration template v1.0. UTF-8 CSV / values-only XLSX. Codes, cheque numbers and references are TEXT. Dates yyyy-MM-dd, LKR amounts signed with 2 decimals, quantities 4 decimals, unit costs 6 decimals. Positive customer/supplier Amount = debt; negative = credit/advance. Export ALL unsettled documents for each party, including offsetting items with a zero net balance. ClosingDate must be the evening before actual go-live (Asia/Colombo). Stable LegacyRecordId strongly recommended. Blank contacts are allowed and flagged. Stock requires all warehouses and FIFO layers, or explicit summarized-layer approval. Cheques: PartyReflected and BankReflected must explicitly state true/false. Received Status = InSafe, Deposited or Endorsed. Issued cheques are pending bank items, not new payments. BankRecon Amount uses bank statement adjustment signs: bank book = statement + outstanding deposits - uncleared payments. Do not add an issued/received cheque a second time as BankRecon. Source trial balance must include every ledger account, including zero balances. Setup accounts, warehouses, UOM, categories, brands and bank branches in normal master maintenance, then save exact mappings in Migration Centre. No historical invoices or receipts are recreated. Empty templates are not evidence of zero; record a signed NotApplicable declaration for modules with no opening balance.
